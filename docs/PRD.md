# TimeAtlas — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name

TimeAtlas

### Tagline

> Explore time around the world.

### Description

TimeAtlas is a modern, minimalist world clock and timezone explorer built with React Native and Expo.

The app allows users to:

- Track multiple cities and time zones
- View current local time
- Understand day/night status
- Explore cities on an interactive world map
- Quickly compare time across locations
- Use the app without requiring a backend

The product is designed to be simple, fast, privacy-conscious, and useful for people who regularly interact across time zones.

---

## 2. Problem

Working, traveling, or communicating across different time zones can make it difficult to know:

- What time it is in another city
- Whether someone is likely awake
- Whether it is a reasonable time to call or message
- How different locations compare
- Where a city is located relative to other time zones

Existing world clock applications can feel outdated, complicated, or overly focused on features that users do not need.

---

## 3. Solution

TimeAtlas provides a simple visual interface for understanding time around the world.

Users can:

1. Add cities they care about.
2. See their current local time.
3. Compare multiple time zones.
4. See whether locations are in day or night.
5. Explore locations on an interactive world map.
6. Quickly identify the current time in any saved location.

### Primary Goal

> Help users understand the right time to connect with people around the world.

---

## 4. Target Users

### Remote Teams

People working with teammates across different countries and time zones.

### Developers

Developers working with international teams, clients, or open-source communities.

### Freelancers

Freelancers communicating with clients in different regions.

### Travelers

People who need to understand local time while traveling.

### Friends & Family

People communicating with friends or family members living in other countries.

### International Businesses

People coordinating activities across multiple offices or regions.

---

## 5. Product Principles

TimeAtlas should follow these principles:

### Simple

The user should understand the application immediately.

### Visual

Timezones should be easy to understand visually through the map and clock list.

### Fast

The application should launch and operate without requiring network requests.

### Offline-first

Core functionality should work without an internet connection.

### Private

The MVP should not require an account or backend.

### Minimal

Avoid unnecessary features and UI complexity.
